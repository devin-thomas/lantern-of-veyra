01 / Lore

The night that stayed

Veyra was built where three old roads meet. For generations, its watchfires were fed by a lantern recovered from the ruins beneath the hill. Then the lantern went dark.

The darkness did not pass with the morning. It stayed. Crops stopped growing, travelers stopped coming, and the town learned to measure time by the coldness of the sky.

The old Guide believes the lantern's heart is still below the sealed Stonewake dungeon. The ruin is guarded by an Ash Dragon and closed behind a rune gate. You are the one traveler willing to take the road down, find the lantern relic, and bring its light back to Veyra.

"The stone remembers the road. The lantern remembers the dawn."
-- The Watcher

03 / Stonewake

Read the landmarks.

The dungeon has one locked idea: every safe landmark is part of the route. The pause screen gives you the next destination.

Full route, coordinates, and boss preparation are hidden until you ask for them.

Rune key
(80,64) Portal
(64,64) Sealed gate
(64,72) Treasure
(48,80) Checkpoint
(80,80) Relic altar
(64,104)

  1. Talk to the Guide.

    Speak with the G GUIDE marker in Veyra. Your objective becomes FIND THE LANTERN RELIC.

  2. Take the crossroads portal.

    The blue portal is at map position (64,64). Stonewake begins on the other side.

  3. Find the Rune Key.

    Search the eastern chamber at (80,64), then return to the sealed gate.

  4. Open the lower halls.

    Use the key at (64,72). The gate changes from a wall to a walkable stone threshold.

  5. Claim the chest.

    At (48,80), take the best reward still missing from your pack, plus 25 gold.

  6. Touch the checkpoint.

    The stone at (80,80) restores HP and MP and records a save.

  7. Wake the altar.

    At (64,104), press A after every lower-hall landmark is complete. The Ash Dragon is waiting.

  8. Carry the light home.

    After victory, return north through the open gate, step onto the portal, and speak with the Guide in Veyra.
