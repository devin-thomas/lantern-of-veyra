Lantern of Veyra01 / The Long Dusk

The Long Dusk

The night has a lock. Find the key. Carry the light home.

Veyra was built where three old roads meet. For generations, its watchfires were fed by a lantern recovered from the ruins beneath the hill. Then the lantern went dark.

The darkness did not pass with the morning. It stayed. Crops stopped growing, travelers stopped coming, and the town learned to measure time by the coldness of the sky.

The old Guide believes the lantern's heart is still below the sealed Stonewake dungeon. The ruin is guarded by an Ash Dragon and closed behind a rune gate. You are the one traveler willing to take the road down, find the lantern relic, and bring its light back to Veyra.

The Guide gives the first direction. The Watcher tells you when you are ready for deeper halls.

Start here

  1. Press A, B, C, or START on NEW GAME.
  2. Walk close to the `G GUIDE` marker.
  3. Press A to accept the quest.
  4. Follow the road to the blue portal.

Inventory and settings

In Inventory, use UP and DOWN to select. A uses a Potion or equips/unequips weapon and armor. B, C, or START closes.

In Settings, use UP and DOWN to select text speed, music, or sound effects. LEFT and RIGHT change the selected value. A, B, or START returns.

Inside the skill menu, A chooses. B, C, or START cancels.

Read the landmarks.

Route order

  1. Guide: Speak with the Guide in Veyra. Your objective becomes FIND THE LANTERN RELIC.
  2. Portal: Enter the blue portal at (64,64).
  3. Rune Key: Search the eastern chamber at (80,64).
  4. Gate: Use the key at (64,72) to open the lower halls.
  5. Treasure: Visit (48,80) for steel, supplies, and 25 gold.
  6. Checkpoint: Touch (80,80) to restore HP/MP and save.
  7. Altar: Reach (64,104) after every lower-hall landmark is complete.
  8. Home: Defeat the Ash Dragon, return north, and speak with the Guide.
The pause screen shows your map position and the next navigation destination.

Town markers

G GUIDE starts and completes the quest. W WATCHER gives advice. S SHOP sells equipment and Potions. I INN restores HP and MP for 10 gold.

Lantern of Veyra04 / Battlecraft

Steel and spellwork.

Normal encounters occur only in Stonewake. The upper halls are calmer than the lower halls. After four movement steps, an encounter may occur; if luck stays quiet, an encounter is forced by the eighth step.

Landmark areas are safe and reset the danger meter. The upper halls have a 10 percent chance after the minimum step count. The lower halls have an 18 percent chance.

Damage

Basic damage is attack minus defense, with a 0-2 damage swing. Damage never falls below 1. A critical hit has a 1 in 16 chance and doubles the result. Guard halves the next enemy hit, rounding up.

Chest order

The Stonewake chest gives the best reward you still lack:

  1. Iron Sword, if you do not have one.
  2. Chain Armor, if the sword is already in your pack.
  3. Three Potions, if both pieces of steel are already yours.

Every first opening also gives 25 gold.

Equipment rule

Buying a weapon or armor does not equip it automatically. Open Inventory and press A on the item you want. Press A again to remove it. One weapon and one armor can be equipped at a time.

The stronger steel is not a shortcut. It is a reason to go back down prepared.

Lantern of Veyra06 / The Guardian

The Ash Dragon.

The relic altar will not wake until the route is complete.

At the altar, press A after the Rune Key, gate, treasure, and checkpoint are all recorded. The Ash Dragon will rise. There is no escape from this battle.

When the flame turns

When the boss reaches half HP, it enters the enraged phase before its next attack. Its ATK rises by 4, its DEF rises by 1, and its Burn lasts three turns instead of two.

Preparation card

  • Touch the checkpoint first.
  • Carry Potions if your stock is low.
  • Use the Iron Sword and Chain Armor when available.
  • Save before the altar.
  • Guard when a burned or weakened hero needs one more turn.
After victory, return north through the open gate. The town portal is still your road home.

Lantern of Veyra07 / Saving

Write the light down.

Press START on the field to write your progress to cartridge SRAM. The game also saves after important interactions, battles, the Stonewake checkpoint, returning to Veyra, and changes made in the pause menus.

If the hero falls in battle, the adventure ends with ALL PROGRESS LOST. Press START to return to the title screen. Save before entering the lower halls and use the checkpoint before the altar.

Valid save data

A valid save stores the current map, position, stats, inventory, equipment, story flags, and audio settings. It includes a version and checksum so invalid data is rejected.

If an emulator asks whether to enable save RAM, enable it.
